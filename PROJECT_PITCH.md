# 🌾 AI Powered Recommendations — KisanConnect

---

## Short Project Description

**KisanConnect** is an AI-powered mobile platform built for Indian farmers that delivers hyper-personalized agronomic intelligence directly to their smartphones. Leveraging Gemini AI, the app recommends the right Syngenta products at the right time based on the farmer's crop, location, and season — then pushes those recommendations via in-app notifications and WhatsApp broadcasts. It layers real-time weather alerts and live mandi price feeds on top, and connects farmers with each other and with Syngenta field agents for peer-to-peer support.

**Target Users:** Smallholder farmers across India, Syngenta field representatives, and agronomic advisors.

**Intended Business Impact:** Drives timely product adoption by reaching farmers during critical crop stages, reduces yield loss through proactive weather and pest alerts, and deepens Syngenta's last-mile farmer engagement — turning the app into a trusted seasonal companion rather than a one-time download.

---

## Problem Statement Being Solved

Indian farmers face three compounding problems every season:

1. **Information lag** — Product recommendations reach farmers too late, after the pest pressure or nutrient deficiency has already caused damage.
2. **Market blindness** — Farmers sell produce without visibility into real-time mandi prices, leaving money on the table.
3. **Isolation** — Smallholders in the same district face the same problems but have no structured channel to share solutions; Syngenta agents cannot physically reach every village at every critical moment.

Existing solutions are fragmented — weather apps don't know what crop the farmer grows, and agri-advisory services don't push timely product recommendations. KisanConnect unifies all three pain points in one AI-driven interface.

## How Does AI/ML Add Value?

| Layer | AI/ML Contribution |
|---|---|
| **Seasonal Product Recommendations** | Gemini AI analyses the farmer's registered crop, current growth stage, geo-location, and historical weather patterns to generate ranked product recommendations with usage guidance — personalised per farmer, not generic broadcast. |
| **WhatsApp & Push Broadcast** | AI determines *when* to send alerts (pre-sowing, flowering, harvest window) so the notification arrives at the agronomically optimal moment, maximising conversion and reducing alert fatigue. |
| **Weather Intelligence** | Real-time weather data is interpreted by the AI layer to generate actionable advisories ("Rain expected in 48 hrs — apply fungicide now") rather than raw numbers. |
| **Market Price Insights** | ML-driven price trend summaries help farmers decide whether to sell immediately or wait, framed in plain regional language. |
| **Farmer Connect Q&A** | Gemini powers a conversational support layer where farmers ask crop questions in Hindi/Tamil/Telugu and receive contextual answers, with the option to escalate to the nearest Syngenta agent or peer farmer. |

Without AI, this is a static bulletin board. With AI, it becomes a personalised farm advisor at scale — one model serving millions of micro-contexts simultaneously.

## Key Features / Modules

### 1. 🔔 Smart Seasonal Notifications
- AI-generated product recommendations pushed as in-app notifications and WhatsApp broadcasts
- Timing engine aligned to crop calendar and local weather triggers
- Supports Hindi, Tamil, and Telugu for regional reach

### 2. 🌦️ Real-Time Weather Reports
- Hyperlocal weather feed integrated with actionable agronomic advisory
- Alerts for extreme events (frost, excess rain, drought stress) with immediate crop protection guidance
- 7-day outlook tied to crop stage

### 3. 📈 Live Market Price Updates
- Real-time mandi prices for major crops in the farmer's district
- AI-generated sell/hold guidance based on price trends
- Price history charts for informed decision-making

### 4. 🤝 Farmer Connect — Peer & Expert Support
- Location-aware farmer community: connect with the nearest farmers growing the same crop
- Post queries, share field photos, exchange solutions
- Syngenta field agents visible on the network for direct escalation
- Gemini AI pre-answers common questions before routing to a human

### 5. 🌱 Personalised Onboarding
- Farmers register crop type, acreage, and location during onboarding
- Profile drives all AI personalisation downstream — one-time setup, continuous value
